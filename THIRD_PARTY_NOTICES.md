# Third-party notices

The MIT license at the repository root applies to the project-authored code.

- The FAudio code used by the Android build appears in `native/obj-O2Y/faudio`, with upstream headers and the zlib license in `native/third-party/FAudio-0c423758`. This source snapshot includes Open Amalur's Android platform changes. Its starting point is FAudio revision [0c423758](https://github.com/FNA-XNA/FAudio/commit/0c423758).
- The Direct3D and Win32 declaration headers in `native/dxvk` are from DXVK's native include tree. The DXVK license and the original Wine notices in individual files apply to those components.
- Vulkan declarations in `native/dependencies/vulkan` are Khronos Vulkan-Headers under Apache-2.0. The header notices and license accompany them.
- The build uses Capstone 5.0.6, LLVM-mingw and the Android NDK from their own distributions. The NDK supplies the NativeActivity glue source at build time.
- The Microsoft and Bink runtime files and the Re-Reckoning executable are local player-provided inputs. Each retains its original terms.
